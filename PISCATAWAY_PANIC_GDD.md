## Backstory

Here's what the Piscataway tourism board doesn't put on the brochure.

In 1943, a covert facility called the **Middlesex Sampling Plant** was built on Mountain Avenue in Middlesex County, NJ. As part of the Manhattan Project, the plant sampled, stored, blended, and transferred uranium, beryllium, and thorium ore for use in the development of atomic weapons. Operations continued well into the Cold War era, finally shutting down in 1967.

Cleanup happened. Mostly.

Over the following decades, a slow uranium leachate crept through the groundwater table of Middlesex County — pooling quietly in low-lying areas like the Raritan River floodplains, the soft earth beneath Johnson Park, and the drainage ditches along South Washington Avenue. Nobody felt it. Nothing happened.

**Until this summer.**

A record drought dropped the water table three feet. A construction crew breaching a buried containment vessel did the rest. A concentrated radioactive plume surfaced simultaneously in six locations across Piscataway.

It didn't kill people. It did something *weirder.*

The radiation activates latent aggression and disrupts higher brain function in anyone who absorbs a sustained dose. The affected aren't dead. They're feral, glowing faintly green, and extremely annoyed. First signs were the Johnson Park animals. Then the Rutgers mascot horse. Then the crowd at the late-season football game.

**Welcome back to Piscataway.**

## Player Character

**Alex "Scoop" Petrucci** — yes, *that* Petrucci family, operators of the beloved Petrucci's Inside Scoop ice cream shop on South Washington Avenue since 1998. Alex was working a Sunday afternoon shift when the first wave hit the line of customers out front.

Armed with whatever's behind the counter, Alex has one goal: survive, find family, and reach the Rutgers emergency broadcast tower on Busch Campus before the whole town glows itself off the map.

**Starting inventory:** One jury-rigged soft serve cannon. One motor scooter (in the parking lot). Zero backup.

-----

## Core Tone

- **Fun and memey.** This is a love letter to people who grew up in Piscataway, graduated, and moved away. Every location, enemy, and item should feel like a winking inside joke.
- **Arcade energy.** Fast, loud, chaotic. Think *House of the Dead* meets *Zombies Ate My Neighbors* with a Central Jersey attitude.
- **Nobody actually dies.** Enemies are knocked out, slip on soft serve, or wander off confused. The violence is Looney Tunes caliber. Keep it PG-13.
- **The goat is fine.** The Johnson Park goat companion (unlocked in Level 2) must never be harmed. He is unbothered. He is eternal.

## Weapons & Mechanics

### 🍦 The Soft Serve Cannon *(Starting Weapon)*

Petrucci's signature soft serve machine, ripped off the counter and jury-rigged with a CO2 canister from the soda fountain.

- **Function:** Fires a continuous stream of soft serve that freezes/slows enemies on contact
- **Upgrade Path:**
  - Tier 1 — Chocolate/Vanilla (baseline stream)
  - Tier 2 — Twist (fires both simultaneously, wider spread)
  - Tier 3 — **The Radioactive Creamsicle** (orange glow; burns AND slows)
- **Mechanic:** Infinite ammo but overheats. Shake device / mash button to cool down.

-----

### 🍕 The Funnel Cake Frisbee *(Found: Petrucci's display case)*

Freshly fried funnel cakes make surprisingly aerodynamic projectiles.

- **Function:** Sticks to enemies; distracts them for 4 seconds while they try to eat it
- **Lore:** Because even zombified, Central Jersey people cannot resist a funnel cake
- **Mechanic:** Limited inventory per level; refills at food stands

-----

### 🐐 The Goat Launcher *(Found: Johnson Park, Level 2)*

Not what it sounds like. The Johnson Park goat — a beloved and completely unbothered survivor — wears a harness with a slingshot apparatus. Load it with rocks, expired zoo food, whatever's nearby.

- **Function:** High damage, zero accuracy. Total chaos.
- **Mechanic:** Goat refuses to be aimed precisely. Trajectory is randomized ±30 degrees.
- **Note:** The goat also functions as a passive companion. He has his own health bar labeled **GOAT INTEGRITY**.

-----

### 🏈 The Scarlet Cannon *(Found: SHI Stadium, Level 3)*

A confetti and T-shirt air cannon from the stadium's game operations closet.

- **Function:** Fires whatever fits — rocks, chain link, commemorative cups. Massive knockback.
- **Upgrade:** Load with actual footballs for wall-ricocheting homing shots
- **Mechanic:** Slow reload (5 seconds). One-shot crowd control.

-----

### ☢️ The Sampling Flask *(Found: Middlesex Sampling Plant ruins)*

A shielded flask of mildly radioactive water sourced from the old contamination site on Mountain Avenue. In-game flavor text calls it the **"Mountain Avenue Margarita."**

- **Function:** Thrown grenade; creates an 8-second "confusion zone" — affected enemies turn on each other
- **Mechanic:** Limited uses per level; refillable at glowing ground puddles

-----

### 🚣 The D&R Canal Paddle *(Found: Canal trail, Level 2)*

A vintage canoe paddle from the Delaware & Raritan Canal State Park towpath, coated in 190-year-old canal sludge.

- **Function:** Pure melee. Wide sweep arc. Sludge counts as a mild toxin — hit enemies reel and react with audible disgust.
- **Mechanic:** Extendable range upgrade available. Satisfying swing animation is non-negotiable.

-----

### 🎺 The Tuba Nuke *(Found: SHI Stadium band room)*

A melee-range sonic weapon salvaged from the Rutgers Marching Band.

- **Function:** Charge for 2 seconds, release a devastating honk that sends nearby enemies flying
- **Mechanic:** Cannot be used indoors without structural consequences (ceiling tiles fall). Camera shakes on use. A crow somewhere always dies. This is not explained.

-----

### 🛵 The Petrucci Scooter *(Environmental — Level 1 only)*

The shop's delivery scooter, still running in the parking lot.

- **Function:** Speed boost / chase sequence vehicle. Controls are intentionally slightly wobbly.
- **Mechanic:** 45-second timed sprint down Washington Avenue. Dodge mutated joggers. Hitting 3+ joggers ends the sequence early.

## Level 1: "Two Scoops, No Waiting"

### Overview

| Property             | Value                                                                                         |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Location**         | Petrucci's Inside Scoop → South Washington Ave → Johnson Park entrance gate                   |
| **Time of Day**      | Sunday afternoon, ~3:47 PM                                                                    |
| **Tone**             | Saturday morning cartoon chaos. Bright colors, comedic enemies, low stakes escalating slowly. |
| **Primary Weapon**   | Soft Serve Cannon (starting)                                                                  |
| **Secondary Find**   | Funnel Cake Frisbee (mid-level)                                                               |
| **Vehicle Sequence** | Petrucci Scooter (two-thirds through)                                                         |

-----

### Setting

A perfect late-summer Sunday in Piscataway. The line at Petrucci's Inside Scoop (474 S. Washington Ave) is out the door — families, kids, guys who are definitely not supposed to be getting ice cream but are. The sun is out. The soft serve machine is humming.

Then the guy at the window starts growling.

Within 90 seconds, it cascades. Alex grabs the soft serve machine off the counter, shouts *"I just cleaned this thing!"*, and the level begins.

-----

### Level Flow

**Section 1: Petrucci's Parking Lot**

The immediate area is chaotic but manageable. Tutorial zone — teaches dodging, soft serve mechanics, and the funnel cake throw.

Enemy types introduced:

- **The Radioactive Dad** — wears a Costco polo, chases relentlessly, telegraphs his charge with a loud "HEY!" before committing
- **The Zombie Crossing Guard** — still holding a stop sign. Button prompt lets Alex grab the sign as a temporary shield.
- **The Lawnmower Guy** — a mutated landscaper doing donuts in the parking lot on a riding mower. Environmental hazard; dodge around him, don't engage directly. He recurs as a running gag in every level.

Key item: **Funnel Cake Frisbee** — found in the display case just inside the Petrucci's window, accessible by smashing the glass.

Easter egg: A whiteboard leaderboard inside the shop lists top customers. The #1 entry reads: `GEORGEWASHINGTON | 1778 | ★★★★★`. It is not explained.

-----

**Section 2: South Washington Avenue**

The level scrolls east. Suburban chaos in full effect. Mutated joggers, a confused mail carrier, a pair of teenagers on bikes who are *somehow still just… biking around* and occasionally get in the way.

Environmental hazards:

- Overturned shopping carts rolling downhill (dodge or push back)
- A fire hydrant hit by the lawnmower guy is spraying water — creates a soft serve freeze combo zone if Alex shoots into it

Midpoint checkpoint: The **Petrucci Scooter** is sitting in a driveway, keys in it. The 45-second scooter chase sequence begins here. Alex rides east down Washington Ave while mutated joggers lunge from both sides. Controls wobble slightly on purpose. Hitting 0-1 joggers: bonus score. Hitting 3+: sequence ends early and Alex has to fight through the rest on foot.

-----

**Section 3: Johnson Park Entrance Gate**

The gate to Johnson Park is closed. A mutated park ranger in full uniform stands guard — not actively hostile, just… blocking the entrance while eating a granola bar very aggressively.

**Puzzle:** Throw a Funnel Cake Frisbee over the fence. The ranger zombie sprints after it. Gate unblocked.

If the player is out of Funnel Cakes: alternate solution is to hit the ranger with three soft serve bursts until they slip, then dash through while the gate swings open.

-----

### Mini-Boss: The Radioactive Mom

A mutated mom pushing a double stroller appears at the park gate entrance. Both kids are completely unaffected and are laughing, clearly enjoying the chaos.

**Rules:**

- The stroller and children **cannot be targeted** — the game physically prevents this. Any attempt redirects aim upward.
- Focus fire on the mom until she's hit with enough soft serve to slip and slow down
- She has three "phases" of charge patterns before she surrenders (sits on a bench, confused, kids still giggling)

**Reward:** She drops a coupon. Collecting it grants a small health refill. The coupon is for 10% off at Petrucci's. In-universe, Alex already works there.

-----

### Level End Transition

Alex reaches the Johnson Park main entrance. The park sprawls ahead — 473 acres of floodplain, picnic groves, and a 18th-century colonial village, all currently in various states of radioactive chaos.

In the distance, barely visible past the tree line: a goat. Eating a fence post. Completely unbothered.

The gate swings open.
